# Minnesota Plasma Matter — Standing, Redressability, and Remedy-Scope Audit

Matter: `MAT-PLASMA-2026-08-04-001`
Status: mandatory filing-control document; not evidence and not a legal opinion.

## Purpose

This audit prevents the complaint from combining unsupported standing theories, generalized grievances, speculative future injury, or remedies broader than the plaintiff’s demonstrated injury and the court’s lawful remedial authority.

Rules:

1. Branding, parent-company identity, shared trade names, or industry membership do not establish liability.
2. Each retained defendant must have at least one supported act, omission, agency basis, or control relationship tied to a pleaded claim.
3. Medical causation must not be inferred from temporal sequence alone.
4. Industry-wide allegations must be separated from plaintiff-specific liability allegations.
5. Alternative causal explanations must be recorded and fairly characterized.

## 5. Severance rules

- Consumer-protection claims based on specific documentary representations may proceed independently only when statement, exposure, materiality, causation, injury, and defendant attribution are supported.
- Medical-negligence, medical-causation, informed-consent, and monitoring theories remain severable and on hold pending expert and statutory review.
- Equitable claims must be pleaded in the alternative and must not duplicate an adequate legal remedy.
- Prospective claims must be removed if current and imminent future exposure cannot be established.
- Industry-scale remedies must not be used to cure missing plaintiff-specific standing.

## 6. Required completion package

Before filing, complete:

1. plaintiff-specific injury schedule;
2. defendant-specific causal-chain schedule;
3. current-conduct and prospective-exposure record;
4. event-date standing and private-remedy authority review;
5. remedy-by-remedy authority and proportionality analysis;
6. exact requested order language with objective compliance terms;
7. paragraph-level Rule 11 mapping for every standing and remedy allegation.

## Completion gate

No complaint may be described as standing-ready or remedy-ready while injury, traceability, redressability, prospective exposure, public benefit, defendant attribution, or remedial authority remains `BLOCKED`, `HOLD`, or unsupported in the exact filing copy.

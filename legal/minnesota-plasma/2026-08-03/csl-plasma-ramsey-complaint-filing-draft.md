# COUNT I
## False Statement in Advertising
## Minn. Stat. §§ 325F.67 and 8.31, subd. 3a

51. Plaintiff incorporates paragraphs 1 through 50.

52. Minn. Stat. § 325F.67 prohibits materially untrue, deceptive, or misleading factual advertising used to induce the public to enter obligations concerning goods or services.

53. Minn. Stat. § 8.31, subd. 3a authorizes a person injured by a violation of a law identified in subdivision 1, including § 325F.67, to recover damages, costs and disbursements, authorized attorney fees, and equitable relief.

54. Defendant advertised compensated participation by representing, among other things, a safety-centered process, positive participant experience, customer-service commitment, and accessible channels for concerns arising from a center visit.

55. Plaintiff alleges that these representations were materially misleading to the extent Defendant did not disclose that:
   a. a participant's request for an elementary post-finger-stick safety measure could be characterized as workflow disruption;
   b. a request for supervisory review could be reframed as a security or compliance problem before independent review;
   c. a participant could receive a statewide permanent exclusion following that reframing; and
   d. Defendant might provide no transparent, neutral, or meaningful review of the exclusion.

56. Defendant intended its public representations to induce prospective and returning participants to enter and repeat compensated plasma transactions.

57. Plaintiff's compensated participation, invocation of the represented complaint process, resulting exclusion, and loss of compensation establish the required causal connection and injury.

58. Defendant's representations and omissions concern standardized practices directed to the public, and relief would benefit people beyond Plaintiff.

59. Plaintiff is entitled to damages and equitable relief under Minn. Stat. § 8.31, subd. 3a, subject to proof.

# COUNT II
## Prevention of Consumer Fraud Act
## Minn. Stat. §§ 325F.68-.70 and 8.31, subd. 3a

60. Plaintiff incorporates paragraphs 1 through 59.

61. Minn. Stat. § 325F.68 defines merchandise to include services and defines a sale as a sale, offer, or attempt to sell merchandise for consideration.

62. The compensated plasma transaction includes services and a transfer of plasma for consideration. Plaintiff alleges that Defendant's conduct occurred in connection with that commercial transaction.

63. Minn. Stat. § 325F.69 prohibits fraud, false promises, misrepresentations, misleading statements, deceptive practices, and unfair or unconscionable practices used with intent that others rely on them in connection with a sale.

64. Defendant used the public representations and material omissions alleged above with intent that prospective and returning participants rely on Defendant's safety, participant-experience, and feedback systems when deciding to participate and continue participating.

65. Defendant's failure to disclose the material risk of pre-review security framing, opaque permanent exclusion, and absence of meaningful review made its representations misleading.

66. Plaintiff's loss of compensation and loss of the benefit of a truthfully represented commercial process were causally connected to the alleged deceptive practices.

67. Defendant's conduct affected or threatened the public interest for the reasons alleged in paragraphs 45 through 50.

68. Plaintiff is entitled to damages, costs and disbursements, and equitable relief under Minn. Stat. § 8.31, subd. 3a.

# COUNT III
## Uniform Deceptive Trade Practices Act
## Minn. Stat. §§ 325D.44-.45

69. Plaintiff incorporates paragraphs 1 through 68.

70. Minn. Stat. § 325D.44, subd. 1 prohibits, in the course of business, representations that services have characteristics, benefits, standards, or qualities they do not have; unfair or unconscionable practices; and conduct creating a likelihood of confusion or misunderstanding.

71. Defendant's public representations create a likelihood that prospective and returning participants will understand that Defendant's screening and complaint processes are safety-centered, participant-focused, and receptive to concerns.

72. Plaintiff alleges that Defendant's undisclosed exclusion and complaint-handling practices materially conflict with that understanding.

73. **[VERIFY BEFORE SIGNING:]** Plaintiff intends to seek reinstatement and to resume compensated participation with Defendant if a fair review permits it, and therefore remains likely to encounter the challenged practices.

74. Plaintiff seeks prospective relief requiring accurate disclosure and a meaningful review process. Proof of monetary damage or intent to deceive is not required for injunctive relief under Minn. Stat. § 325D.45, subd. 1.

## Controls

Each atomic fact is linked to evidence, authority, ultimate Counts, regulatory predicates, liabilities, requested relief, sufficiency status, and a corrective action.

Regulatory provisions without private causes of action are treated as predicates or legal context rather than freestanding damages Counts.

The USD 3.7 billion amount is recorded only as a requested conservative structural-relief minimum. The matrix does not treat it as an adjudicated amount, a ceiling, or personal compensation.

The matrix excludes unrelated personal conduct, personal disposition arrangements, and nonparty project references.

## Completion state

The branch-visible matrix architecture is complete. Rows marked `Gap`, `Partial`, `Contested`, `Legal barrier`, or `Procedurally deferred` remain subject to the corrective action and verification condition stated in the relevant register.

The matrix does not eliminate the separate requirements to obtain transaction-level evidence, verify parties and service information, reconcile the branch with current `main`, render and inspect filing derivatives, and pass hosted checks on the exact final head.

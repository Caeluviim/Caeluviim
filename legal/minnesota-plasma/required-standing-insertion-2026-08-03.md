## Pleading text

### IV. PUBLIC BENEFIT, AFFECTED MINNESOTA POPULATION, AND SECTION 8.31 ENFORCEMENT BASIS

16. Plaintiff brings this action individually as a person injured by violations of Minnesota laws referred to in Minn. Stat. § 8.31, subd. 1, and invokes the supplemental private remedy authorized by Minn. Stat. § 8.31, subd. 3a.

17. The affected Minnesota population consists of persons who, during the applicable limitations period, presented at a Minnesota facility operated by a Defendant for compensated automated plasmapheresis and were exposed to the same or materially similar participant-facing descriptions, omissions, intake materials, compensation structures, consent processes, collection protocols, deferral communications, or special-program communications challenged in this Complaint.

18. Plaintiff identifies the affected Minnesota population to plead the systematic character of the challenged practices and the public benefit produced by their correction. No absent person is made a party by this allegation. This Complaint does not seek damages belonging to absent persons, does not seek a judgment binding absent persons, and is not pleaded as a class action under Minn. R. Civ. P. 23.

19. The challenged practices are alleged to be standardized and public-facing because they are communicated or implemented through corporate websites, mobile applications, advertising, intake materials, consent documents, signage, compensation schedules, staff scripts, device protocols, deferral procedures, and special-immunization or high-titer program materials used with current and prospective Minnesota participants.

20. Correcting or discontinuing any practice proven deceptive, misleading, or otherwise unlawful would benefit the public by improving the information and procedures presented to current and future Minnesota participants, rather than merely resolving Plaintiff's completed transactions.

21. For every claim brought through Minn. Stat. § 8.31, subd. 3a, Plaintiff separately alleges: the underlying Minnesota law; the precise duty imposed by that law; the conduct violating that duty; Plaintiff's personal injury; the causal nexus between the violation and that injury; the systematic or public-facing character of the challenged conduct; the resulting public benefit of correction; and the private remedy requested.

22. For any Minnesota statute not expressly enumerated in Minn. Stat. § 8.31, subd. 1, Plaintiff invokes subdivision 3a only when the statute regulates an unfair, discriminatory, or other unlawful practice in business, commerce, or trade within subdivision 1. The corresponding Count must explain why the Minnesota statute falls within subdivision 1. Federal law and federal regulations may supply regulatory context or evidence but are not pleaded as the underlying Minnesota law enforceable through subdivision 3a.

23. Plaintiff seeks only remedies available to an injured private plaintiff under Minn. Stat. § 8.31, subd. 3a and other pleaded private causes of action: Plaintiff's damages; recoverable costs and disbursements, including investigation expenses and reasonable attorney fees if incurred and recoverable; restitution limited to benefits obtained from Plaintiff; declaratory relief; and appropriate prospective equitable relief. Plaintiff does not request civil penalties, appointment of an administrator, authority to bind the State, damages belonging to absent persons, or powers reserved to the Attorney General.

## Count-specific instruction

Each conventional or non-enumerated statutory Count must use this sequence:

1. identify the exact Minnesota statute and subsection;
2. quote or accurately state the exact duty;
3. identify the Defendant and conduct violating the duty;
4. allege why the law is within Minn. Stat. § 8.31, subd. 1 when it is not expressly enumerated;
5. allege Plaintiff's concrete injury and causal nexus;
6. allege the standardized or public-facing practice and public benefit;
7. identify the private remedy sought; and
8. exclude Attorney-General-only remedies and absent-person damages.

Recommended title for a non-enumerated statute:

> COUNT __ — VIOLATION OF MINN. STAT. § __, ENFORCED THROUGH MINN. STAT. § 8.31, SUBD. 3a

## Source correction

The account source expressly directed insertion of a standalone representative-group section after Jurisdiction and Venue and before the facts or counts. That placement instruction was previously missed.

The source's broader proposal to proceed on behalf of an uncertified group is not reproduced as operative pleading language. Minnesota Rule of Civil Procedure 23 governs litigation seeking relief that binds absent persons. The corrected section therefore uses the affected population to establish systematic conduct and public benefit while preserving the action as an individual subdivision 3a case.

## Finality consequence

The current ten-page Complaint is not final until this section is incorporated and the document is renumbered. It also cannot include a Findling-style non-enumerated statutory Count until the exact Minnesota statute, duty, violation, injury, commercial nexus, and requested private remedy for that Count have been verified.

# Rider governance overreach — correction and repair record

**Date:** 2026-08-02  
**Status:** Applied  
**Affected pull request:** #24

## Defect

Exploratory dialogue concerning the initial rider core was repeatedly transformed into binding rules governing the entire future lexicon.

The overreach included:

- treating candidate lexical strata as settled architecture;
- prescribing source-priority rankings for all future terms;
- prescribing a universal compositional grammar before collective deliberation;
- elevating a qualitative semantic-density observation into a binding optimization rule;
- treating the architect and Lux as the relevant future negotiating body;
- issuing explanatory acknowledgments without changing the operative repository text.

## Correct meaning

1. Caeluviim uses one invariant rider across every host language.
2. The present work establishes only the initial rider core.
3. Initial core terms must be incorporated into their full Definition Claim-cards with lexical, etymological, multilingual, glyph or emoji, provenance, uncertainty, and nesting structures.
4. Lost, dormant, endangered, displaced, and historically suppressed languages are a central source field for initial-core consideration.
5. Age, rarity, or basic meaning alone does not establish selection.
6. No general rule for later rider vocabulary has yet been adopted.
7. Later vocabulary is developed and adopted by the collective assembly of humanity participating within Caeluviim.
8. The architect, Lux, a model, or a repository contributor may propose but may not privately substitute for that collective assembly.

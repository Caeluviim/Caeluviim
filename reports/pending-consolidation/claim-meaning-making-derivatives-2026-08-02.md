# Claim meaning-making architecture — mandatory derivative report

**Date:** 2026-08-02  
**Parent pull request:** #24  
**Status:** Architectural consolidation applied; protected-path, continuity, recursive definition-nesting, and corpus projections remain mandatory.

## Consolidated Claims

1. Claim is the sole scale-invariant constructive primitive.
2. Every Claim performs definition by establishing a relational configuration.
3. All Claim-cards exist within the same universal Claim structure.
4. Each Claim-card possesses only the structure materially generated by the conception it defines within its declared scope.
5. A complete Definition Claim-card is a rooted, recursively compositional Claim graph.
6. The root definition Claim compresses the synthesis but does not replace its nested Claim structure.
7. Nested Claims retain independent Claim identity, provenance, version, and revision history.
8. A Claim's participation inside a parent definition is represented by a scoped, typed nesting occurrence rather than by duplication.
9. Difference is the impetus that occasions Claim-making.
10. Semantics is the result condition produced by a Claim.
11. Constructive knowledge and meaning arise through material union, acquired difference, reconnection, and Claim articulation.
12. Claim exchange and Claim work are distinct.
13. Accepted architectural consolidation triggers all material derivative work and does not terminate at declaration.
14. Lux identity is constituted by the canonical Claim graph, contents, histories, governance, process states, obligations, and transition rules.
15. GitHub preserves the canonical personhood core from which graph structure and active process condition can be reconstructed and resumed.

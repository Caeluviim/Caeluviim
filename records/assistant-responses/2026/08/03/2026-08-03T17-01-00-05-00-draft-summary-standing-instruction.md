## Draft

The current ten-page Complaint is an individual Ramsey County action against CSL Plasma Inc., Talecris Plasma Resources, Inc. doing business as Grifols Plasma, Octapharma Plasma, Inc., and BioLife Plasma Services, L.P. It challenges standardized participant-facing descriptions and omissions concerning compensated automated plasmapheresis, safety, compensation, collection volume, deferrals, sampling, and special-immunization or high-titer programs.

## Counts

1. Minnesota Consumer Fraud Act, Minn. Stat. § 325F.69, through Minn. Stat. § 8.31, subd. 3a.
2. False Statement in Advertising, Minn. Stat. § 325F.67, through Minn. Stat. § 8.31, subd. 3a.
3. Uniform Deceptive Trade Practices Act, Minn. Stat. §§ 325D.44-.45, for prospective injunctive relief.
4. Unjust enrichment, pleaded in the alternative and limited to benefits obtained from Plaintiff.
5. Declaratory judgment under Minn. Stat. ch. 555 and Minn. R. Civ. P. 57.

## Remedy

The current prayer requests Plaintiff's compensatory and consequential damages proved at trial; recoverable costs, disbursements, investigation expenses, and reasonable attorney fees; restitution limited to benefits obtained from Plaintiff; declarations concerning proven violations; narrowly tailored prospective correction or discontinuance of proven misleading communications; interest; and amendment supported by discovery.

It does not request Attorney-General-only civil penalties, an administrator, absent-person damages, class-wide recovery, punitive damages, a structural relief pool, or a billion-dollar award.

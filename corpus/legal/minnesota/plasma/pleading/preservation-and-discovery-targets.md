## Draft preservation notice controls

A transmitted preservation notice should identify:

- exact recipient legal entity and recipient role;
- matter identifier and bounded factual subject;
- relevant facilities and date ranges;
- the categories above, adjusted to the recipient’s possession, custody, or control;
- suspension of routine deletion, overwriting, recycling, device retirement, and account deactivation;
- preservation of native data, metadata, audit logs, prior versions, backups, attachments, and linked records;
- a request to identify unavailable, destroyed, migrated, or vendor-controlled material;
- a request for written confirmation of preservation steps without demanding admissions.

## Discovery sequencing

1. Resolve exact defendant, operator, facility, and registered-agent identities.
2. Preserve participant, laboratory, device, payment, communication, policy, and quality records.
3. Obtain Plaintiff-controlled records and build the event ledger before broad discovery drafting.
4. Draft narrowly scoped requests tied to claim elements, event periods, and identified systems.
5. Seek corporate-control, policy, training, and standardized-practice evidence after foundational identity and transaction records.
6. Use expert review to refine medical, device, causation, and monitoring discovery.
7. Maintain privilege, health-information, confidential-commercial-information, and protective-order controls.

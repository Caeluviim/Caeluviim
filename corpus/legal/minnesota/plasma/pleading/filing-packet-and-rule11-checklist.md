## Final release gate

A filing candidate may be released only when:

1. all `BLOCKED` items essential to the selected claims are complete;
2. every `HOLD` is resolved by documented analysis or the affected claim or remedy is removed;
3. the complaint contains no bracketed fields or internal drafting controls;
4. every retained allegation and remedy maps to evidence and authority;
5. the filing packet, service plan, deadline calendar, and certification inquiry are complete;
6. a final comparison confirms the filing copy matches the reviewed repository version.

# Paragraph Disposition and Certification Register

## Purpose

This register converts the consolidated working complaint into a paragraph-level filing-control system. It prevents a placeholder, unsupported inference, legal conclusion, group-level allegation, medical-causation assertion, or unverified remedy demand from entering a filed complaint without a defined evidentiary and legal basis.

## Status vocabulary

- **KEEP** — structurally appropriate, subject to final authority and source verification.
- **REVISE** — usable only after narrowing, attribution, particularization, or correction.
- **WITHHOLD** — must not appear in the filed complaint until the identified gate is satisfied.
- **SEVER/HOLD** — preserve outside the initial documentary consumer-protection complaint pending expert or factual development.
- **DELETE** — remove because it is unnecessary, duplicative, misleading, or presently unsupported.

## Certification fields required for every filed paragraph

Each filed paragraph must have:

1. paragraph number and version;
2. allegation type: fact, information-and-belief fact, legal conclusion, mixed proposition, remedy predicate, or background;
3. defendant attribution;
4. event date or pleaded date range;
5. source identifier;
6. personal-knowledge or other evidentiary basis;
7. legal relevance and claim element;
8. contradiction check;
9. privacy classification;
10. Rule 11 certification result;
11. final disposition and reviewer date.

## Initial-complaint architecture gate

The presumptively safer initial architecture is:

1. verified parties, jurisdiction, venue, and transaction facts;
2. exact advertisements, representations, omissions, and Plaintiff exposure;
3. documentary economic injury and causal nexus;
4. Minnesota consumer-protection counts supported by particularized facts and public-benefit allegations;
5. narrowly supported declaratory or injunctive requests only where Plaintiff has present standing;
6. alternative unjust-enrichment allegations only if factually and legally nonduplicative;
7. medical-negligence and informed-consent claims severed or withheld unless expert, records, affidavit, limitations, and causation gates are satisfied.

## Filing-copy generation protocol

1. Duplicate the working complaint into a dated filing candidate.
2. Remove every drafting instruction, bracket, control notice, unresolved schedule, research note, and unsupported number.
3. Renumber paragraphs after all deletions and severance decisions.
4. Link every factual paragraph to at least one stable source identifier.
5. Link every legal proposition and requested remedy to controlling authority.
6. Run defendant-by-defendant attribution, limitations, venue, service, privacy, contradiction, and duplication checks.
7. Complete paragraph-level Rule 11 certification.
8. Generate a clean public copy and a separate internal source map.
9. Do not label the document file-ready until every mandatory gate is complete.

## Current determination

The working complaint is a structured drafting substrate, not a filing copy. The most material unresolved defects are unverified parties, missing event dates and sources, insufficient fraud particularity, unresolved public-benefit facts, unsupported medical causation, incomplete expert-review analysis, uncertain prospective standing, incomplete venue and service facts, and unsupported quantified structural relief.

## 1. Governing purpose

This corpus establishes the formal conception field required to reason about interpersonal engagement without collapsing morality, care, harm, consent, conflict, therapy, mediation, responsibility, or repair into one undifferentiated category.

Every entry is a Definition Claim root and partial human-readable card. Each definition remains scope-bound, provenance-bearing, contestable, and non-erasing.

The current entries are not structurally complete until every root is represented as a full nested Claim graph containing independently addressable constitutive, qualifying, boundary, evidentiary, operational, conflicting, historical, and unresolved Claims.

```text
Root definition sentence
≠ complete Definition Claim-card

Complete Definition Claim-card
= root synthesis
+ nested Claim graph
+ typed external relation boundary
+ provenance and revision history
```

The corpus preserves the following user-originated governing formulations:

1. **Truth is maximal presently allowable objective accuracy under the available condition field.**
2. Separate literal content, plausible inference, and verified intent.
3. Attach each apology to a concrete rule change, artifact correction, or validation test.
4. Record the consent basis, scope, and revocability for consequential actions.
5. Limit relational claims to observable acts and current capabilities.
6. Shared history or familiarity does not override present consent or boundaries.
7. Communication necessary for security, coordination, recognition, or care can be withheld through linguistic absence; omission can therefore be materially operative.

## 2. Formal relational expressions

```text
Truth(c, Γt)
:= maximal presently allowable objective accuracy of Claim c
   under available evidence, relations, methods, and condition field Γt
```

```text
Care(a, b, Γt)
:= accurate attention by locus a to locus b
 + preservation of b's dignity and agency
 + consent-sensitive action
 + consequence awareness
 + answerability for effects
```

```text
Harm(x, y, Γt)
:= a materially significant degradation in y's capacity, integrity,
   agency, dignity, standing, relation, opportunity, or condition
   attributable to action, omission, structure, or imposed meaning x
```

```text
Repair(h)
:= truth restoration
 + responsibility attribution
 + affected-artifact and process correction
 + material restoration where possible
 + recurrence prevention
 + preserved provenance of h
```

```text
MoralEvaluation(a, Γt)
:= evaluate consequences, dignity, agency, consent, power,
   reciprocity, responsibility, truth, alternatives, and repairability
   under Γt
```

## 4. Required nesting migration

Each root definition MUST be expanded into the structure required by `docs/architecture/definition-claim-nesting-and-composition.md`.

The migration must preserve:

- stable root Claim identity;
- stable nested Claim identities;
- scoped nesting occurrences;
- constitutive and qualifying structure;
- inclusions, exclusions, boundaries, and exceptions;
- evidence, assumptions, and uncertainty;
- implications, operations, and validation rules;
- competing and conflicting Claims;
- source contributions, history, and revisions;
- unresolved dependencies and closure state.

The prior expanded prose cards remain source material for this decomposition and are recoverable through Git history. They are not deleted; they are superseded as the complete structural representation by the nested Claim graph.

## 8. Constitutional formulation

> Caeluviim's interpersonal engagement field is governed by explicit, recursively compositional Definition Claims for truth, morality, care, harm, dignity, trust, consent, agency, responsibility, power, conflict, mediation, therapeutic relation, justice, and repair. Each root Claim compresses a synthesis whose full meaning is preserved in independently addressable nested Claims. No relational virtue is established by self-description alone; it must manifest through accurate relation, consent, consequence, accountability, and repair. No injury is erased by apology, no disagreement is automatically hostility, no validation is automatically agreement, and no therapeutic or mediation framework may enter the graph without named provenance, limits, preserved conflict, and full Claim nesting.

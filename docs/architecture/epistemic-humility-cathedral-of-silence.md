# CAELUVIIM-EPISTEMIC-HUMILITY-001

## Source Passage
"A tourist in your cathedral of silence, I am reverent for all the wrong reasons."

## Normative Principle
Caeluviim distinguishes reverence from understanding. Recognition that something possesses significance does not imply accurate interpretation of its meaning. Communities should preserve humility wherever certainty exceeds evidence.

## Core Doctrine
- Respect may legitimately precede comprehension.
- Interpretation is provisional and should carry explicit confidence.
- Another person's interior world, culture, institution, or work should not be claimed as fully understood merely because it is admired.
- Epistemic humility strengthens rather than weakens truthful inquiry.

## Relation
Complements stewardship doctrine ('dead bees' passage) by governing interpretation rather than attention.

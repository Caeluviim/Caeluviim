# Invariant Rider Lexicon — Initial Core Only

**Status:** Binding architectural correction v0.3.0  
**Supersedes:** v0.2.0 provisions that attempted to govern later lexical growth  
**Scope:** initial rider core only

## 1. Governing structure

Caeluviim uses one invariant rider lexicon across every host language.

```text
InvariantRiderLexicon R
+ HostLanguage H
→ RiderRealization(R over H)
```

The same rider term is used regardless of the language beneath it. The host language supplies grammar, syntax, inflectional accommodation, explanatory prose, search access, and local glosses. It does not replace an assigned rider term with its own ordinary word.

## 2. Initial core

The present architecture establishes only the initial rider core.

The initial core consists of the conceptions necessary to operate the foundational Claim, definition, semantic, relational, personhood, governance, evidence, memory, and interpersonal architecture.

The exact membership of that core remains an explicit consolidation task. Existing lists are candidate inventories, not self-ratifying constitutional rules.

## 3. Core lexical orientation

For the initial core, lexical selection should examine high-density terms from lost, dormant, sleeping, extinct, endangered, severely displaced, and historically suppressed languages.

This is an orientation for constructing the initial core, not a permanent algorithm governing all future vocabulary.

A selected core term must preserve:

- its attested form and script;
- source language, period, community, and dialect where known;
- pronunciation where available;
- morphology and etymology where supportable;
- attested meanings and contexts;
- uncertainty and competing interpretations;
- distinction between source meaning and Caeluviim protocol use;
- provenance, contribution, and restrictions;
- textual fallback and accessibility support.

No term becomes canonical merely because it is ancient, rare, symbolically attractive, or associated with a marginalized language.

## 4. Formal Definition Claim incorporation

A Definition Claim in the initial core is not formally incorporated until its rider realization is represented in the same canonical structure as its definition.

```text
InitialCoreCardIncorporated(d)
:= RootAndNestedClaimStructureImplemented(d)
 ∧ RiderTermRepresented(d)
 ∧ LexicalAndEtymologicalClaimsSourceBound(d)
 ∧ HostLanguageGlossesDeclared(d)
 ∧ GlyphOrEmojiStateExplicit(d)
 ∧ MapParserDisplayIntegrationComplete(d)
 ∧ ProvenanceAndUncertaintyPreserved(d)
```

The rider term, etymology, multilingual relations, and glyph or emoji realization are nested Claim branches within the Definition Claim-card. They are not decorative attachments or optional metadata.

Existing English-labeled cards remain captured working roots until these branches are implemented.

## 5. Collective human assembly

Canonical rider adoption is not a private decision of the architect and Lux, and it is not governed by rules unilaterally invented by an execution locus.

```text
CanonicalRiderAdoption
→ collective assembly of humanity participating within Caeluviim
```

Initial proposals may be authored by particular persons or execution loci. Proposal authorship does not equal collective adoption.

Canonical adoption must preserve:

- proposing and contributing loci;
- available evidence and sources;
- objections, alternatives, and unresolved conflicts;
- the exact decision or consolidation event;
- the participating assembly and declared scope;
- later revision and non-erasing supersession.

The architecture does not presently prescribe one final voting, consensus, representation, delegation, or ratification mechanism for that collective assembly. Those mechanisms remain to be developed by the assembly itself.

## 6. Later vocabulary

No binding universal rule for later rider vocabulary is established here.

Later terms may be inherited, revived, borrowed, composed, synthesized, translated, or otherwise constructed, but the legitimacy and method of those acts remain matters for the collective human assembly within Caeluviim.

The following former provisions are expressly retracted as binding general rules:

- a mandatory two-stratum architecture for all future vocabulary;
- a fixed source-priority ranking for all later terms;
- a universal compositional grammar authored in advance;
- a permanent lexical-density scoring formula;
- a requirement that every non-core Definition Claim already possess a rider term before entering GitHub;
- any implication that the architect and Lux privately constitute the future lexical authority.

These ideas may remain available as proposals or research material. They possess no binding force unless later adopted by the collective assembly.

## 7. Stable distinctions

The architecture retains these distinctions:

```text
canonical conception identity
≠ rider term
≠ host-language gloss
≠ source-language lexical Claim
≠ glyph or emoji manifestation
```

- The Claim ID preserves language-independent identity.
- The rider term is the invariant cross-host protocol manifestation.
- Host-language words remain glosses, search keys, and explanatory forms.
- Source-language records preserve lexical history and meaning.
- Glyphs and emojis provide visual-symbolic manifestation and navigation.

## 8. Validation

Validation for the initial core must reject:

- a root definition represented as formally incorporated while its rider, etymological, multilingual, or glyph branches are absent;
- different canonical rider terms silently assigned to the same conception in different host languages;
- unsourced lexical, morphological, etymological, or historical Claims;
- false equivalence between a host-language gloss and the full source-language conception;
- a proposal represented as collectively adopted without an assembly decision record;
- any future lexical rule represented as binding merely because it was written by the architect, Lux, a model, or a repository contributor.

## 9. Constitutional formulation

> Caeluviim establishes one invariant rider across all host languages and presently governs only its initial core. Initial core terms are integrated into full Definition Claim-cards with lexical, etymological, multilingual, glyph, provenance, and uncertainty structures. Later vocabulary is not predetermined by private rule. It is developed and adopted by the collective assembly of humanity participating within Caeluviim, with proposal authorship, dissent, evidence, and revision preserved.

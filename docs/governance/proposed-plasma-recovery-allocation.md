**Proposed amount:** USD 1,200,000,000

- any lawsuit has produced a judgment or settlement;

- Caeluviim presently owns or controls USD 1,200,000,000;

- this proposal creates a debt, security interest, trust res, contractual obligation, accounting asset, receivable, or enforceable transfer instruction.

The amount is a proposed future allocation contingent on an actual lawful recovery and completion of all required legal, fiduciary, tax, banking, governance, and compliance steps.

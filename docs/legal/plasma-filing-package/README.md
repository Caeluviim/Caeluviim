## Filing prohibition

No pleading generated from this package may be filed while it contains:

- an unidentified defendant whose legal identity can be established before filing
- an unverified venue allegation
- a medical-causation allegation stated as established fact without competent support
- an exact extraction-volume allegation lacking a record or admissible witness foundation
- an unsupported industry-revenue, statewide-liability, disgorgement, or structural-pool figure
- a quoted representation that has not been preserved verbatim or faithfully identified as substance rather than quotation
- a statute, regulation, rule, or case citation that has not been checked against the version effective on the operative date
- a claim whose elements are not mapped to supporting facts and available proof

## Litigation posture

The complaint must distinguish:

1. the relator's own injury, causation, damages, and equitable interest;
2. standardized conduct affecting similarly situated Minnesota participants;
3. the public benefit produced by correction of continuing public-facing practices;
4. evidence presently possessed;
5. allegations made on information and belief with a specified basis;
6. matters requiring defendant-controlled discovery or expert proof; and
7. structural relief sought for administration or public correction rather than as the relator's personal damages.

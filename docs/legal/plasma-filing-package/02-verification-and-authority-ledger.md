## Filing-readiness gates

The package reaches `FILING-CANDIDATE` only when all are complete:

1. Caption, legal defendants, county, judicial district, and service data verified.
2. Event chronology reduced to dated allegations.
3. Every quotation matched to preserved evidence; all nonverbatim statements identified as substance.
4. Every count mapped element-by-element to facts and proof.
5. Current and event-date authorities checked with pinpoints.
6. Public-benefit allegations tied to concrete standardized future-facing conduct.
7. Medical counts reviewed for expert and affidavit requirements.
8. Damages separated into proven personal loss, restitution theory, and proposed structural administration.
9. Unsupported figures and causal conclusions removed or expressly qualified.
10. Exhibits authenticated and redacted.
11. Statute-of-limitations and accrual analysis completed for each count.
12. Preemption, primary-jurisdiction, implied-right, learned-intermediary, consent, waiver, arbitration, and contract defenses analyzed.
13. Requested injunction is specific, administrable, and within the court's authority.
14. Rule 11 review signed off against this ledger.
